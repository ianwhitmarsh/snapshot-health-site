Security & Trust

Protecting sensitive health information is a core responsibility at Snapshot Health. We invest continuously in our people, technology, and processes to help safeguard the information entrusted to us.

Snapshot Health has completed a SOC 2 Type II examination, providing independent validation of the controls and processes we use to protect customer and patient information.

We are committed to meeting applicable HIPAA requirements and maintaining strong safeguards for the privacy, security, and confidentiality of protected health information.

Security is not a one-time certification or project. We continually evaluate our systems, policies, and practices as our platform and the security landscape evolve.
